# Enhanced Quest System Design - V2.0
## Addressing Long-term Engagement & Movement Variety

### Core Problems Identified
1. **Limited Movement Variety**: Only 6 movement categories will become repetitive
2. **Lack of Progression Narrative**: No story or achievement system to maintain engagement
3. **Missing Social Elements**: No community features or competition
4. **Static Quest Structure**: Same format daily without seasonal variety

### Enhanced Movement System

#### Expanded Movement Categories (18 Total)
Based on calisthenics research, we need more movement patterns:

**Upper Body (8 categories)**
- PULL_VERTICAL: Pull-ups, Chin-ups, Muscle-ups
- PULL_HORIZONTAL: Rows, Reverse flies
- PUSH_VERTICAL: Handstand push-ups, Pike push-ups
- PUSH_HORIZONTAL: Push-ups, Dips
- PUSH_UNILATERAL: Single-arm variations
- PULL_UNILATERAL: Single-arm rows/pulls
- UPPER_ISOMETRIC: Planches, Front levers
- UPPER_DYNAMIC: Explosive movements, plyometrics

**Lower Body (5 categories)**
- SQUAT_BILATERAL: Standard squats, jump squats
- SQUAT_UNILATERAL: Pistol squats, lunges
- HINGE_BILATERAL: Deadlift variations
- HINGE_UNILATERAL: Single-leg deadlifts
- LOWER_PLYOMETRIC: Jump training, agility

**Core & Mobility (5 categories)**
- CORE_STATIC: Planks, hollow holds
- CORE_DYNAMIC: Sit-ups, leg raises
- CORE_ROTATIONAL: Russian twists, wood chops
- FLEXIBILITY: Stretching routines
- MOBILITY_FLOW: Movement chains, yoga flows
